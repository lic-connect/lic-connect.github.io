Yuva Term

Plan No. 875

Maximum Protection for Young Achievers.

YUVA TERM (875) is a Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Individual, Pure Risk Life Insurance Plan. It offers high life cover at affordable rates, specifically designed for young adults to secure their financial future.

Policy Term15 to 40 Years
Entry Age18 to 45 Years (Last B'day)
Min. Sum Assured₹50,00,000
Max. Sum Assured₹5 Cr (Higher on case-to-case)
Premium ModesRegular, Limited, Single
Payment FrequencyYearly, Half-Yearly

Death Benefit Options

The proposer must choose one of the following options at the proposal stage; the choice cannot be altered later:

Option I: Level Sum Assured
The Absolute Amount Assured on Death remains the same throughout the policy term.

Option II: Increasing Sum Assured
Sum Assured remains constant for the first 5 years. From the 6th to 15th year, it increases by 10% of Basic SA annually until it doubles. It remains constant (at 2x Basic SA) from the 16th year onwards.

Sum Assured on Death Definition

Regular/Limited Premium: Highest of 7 times Annualized Premium, 105% of all premiums paid, or the Absolute Amount Assured.

Single Premium: Higher of 125% of Single Premium or the Absolute Amount Assured.
