Guaranteed Survival Benefit

8% of Basic Sum Assured is payable annually on survival.

  • Starts from the end of the Premium Paying Term.
  • Continues every year until maturity (Age 100) or death.
  • This benefit is tax-free.

Death Benefit

  • Before Commencement of Risk: Refund of total premiums paid without interest.
  • After Commencement of Risk: Sum Assured on Death + Vested Simple Reversionary Bonuses + Final Additional Bonus (if any).

"Sum Assured on Death" is the highest of:
1. Basic Sum Assured
2. 7 times of Annualized Premium
3. 105% of all Premiums paid as on date of death

Riders & Maturity

Maturity Benefit (at Age 100):

  • Sum Assured + Vested Bonuses + FAB (if any).

Riders Available:

  • Accidental Death & Disability Benefit: Available up to Age 70.
  • Accident Benefit Rider: Available during PPT only.

Additional Information

Loan: Available after payment of 3 full years' premiums.
Surrender: Allowed after payment of 3 full years' premiums.
Tax Benefit: Premiums eligible under Sec 80C. Maturity/Survival benefits free under Sec 10(10D).
