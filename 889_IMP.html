Key Joint Life Rules (Plan 889 - Jeevan Sathi Limited Premium):
• 1st Death: Sum Assured on Death is paid to the surviving spouse, and all future base premiums are completely waived. The policy continues on the life of the surviving spouse without any further base premium payment.
• 2nd Death: Sum Assured on Death plus full accrued Guaranteed Additions up to the year of death is paid, and the policy terminates.
• Simultaneous Death of Both: Sum of 1st and 2nd death benefits (Twice Sum Assured on Death + accrued GA) is paid to nominees.
• Guaranteed Additions: Accrues annually at 7% of Total Tabular Annual Premium in respect of premiums paid throughout the entire policy term (even after PPT has ended).
• Net Cashflow: Calculated as Total Outflow minus Total Inflow (Positive indicates net outflow / payment made; negative indicates net inflow / maturity receipt).
