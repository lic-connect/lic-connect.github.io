Plan No. 886: The Story of Mr. Vikram’s "Wealth Fortress"

Imagine you are building a "Wealth Fortress". You add bricks (money) every year, but you also want a guard (Insurance) to protect it. This is LIC’s Wealth Plus (Plan 886). Here is how an agent explains it to Mr. Vikram (Age 35), who invests ₹1,00,000 every year for 20 years:

  1. The Construction Fee (PAC): Before Vikram's money starts building the fortress, LIC takes a small fee. In Year 1, this is 8% (for offline).
    Math: ₹1,00,000 - ₹8,000 = ₹92,000 invested in the market.
  2. Buying the Bricks (Units): If the market price (NAV) is ₹10, Vikram gets his first batch of "bricks."
    Math: ₹92,000 ÷ ₹10 = 9,200 Units credited.
  3. The Guard & Upkeep (Charges): LIC provides a ₹10,00,000 Life Cover. Every month, the "Guard" needs a salary. Instead of asking for cash, LIC cancels a few units.
    Math: If monthly charges are ₹200 and NAV is ₹12, LIC cancels 16.6 units.
  4. Reinforcements (Guaranteed Additions): This is the special part of Plan 886! At the end of years 6, 10, 15, 20, and 25, LIC adds extra free units to the fortress as a loyalty bonus.
    Math: At Year 10, LIC might add 10% of premium = ₹10,000 worth of extra units.
  5. Final Fortress Value: At the end of 20 years, Vikram’s total units are multiplied by the final market price (NAV) to give him his maturity.

Key Technical Definitions

1. NAV (Net Asset Value): The "Current Price" of one unit of your fund. It is the unit price you see on the news or LIC website daily.

2. PAC (Premium Allocation Charge): The fee taken from your premium before investment. In Plan 886, it is 8% (Yr 1), 5.5% (Yr 2-5), and 3% (Yr 6+).

3. Admin Charges: A monthly fee for managing the policy. In this plan, it usually starts from the 6th policy year onwards.

4. Mortality Charges: The cost of your Life Cover. It is calculated based on your age and the difference between your sum assured and fund value.

5. FMC (Fund Management Charge): The 1.35% annual fee for LIC’s investment experts who manage the stocks and bonds, adjusted in the NAV daily.

6. GA (Guaranteed Additions): Extra amounts (as a % of annual premium) added to your unit fund at specific milestones (6, 10, 15, 20, 25 years).

How Each Charge is Calculated

  • PAC: Deducted directly from the premium. Formula: `Premium × PAC %`.
  • Mortality Charge: `(Sum at Risk × Mortality Rate for Age) / 12000`. (Deducted monthly by cancelling units).
  • Admin Charge: A fixed monthly amount (e.g., ₹100) divided by the current NAV to determine how many units to cancel.
  • FMC: 1.35% per year. Calculated daily: `(Current Fund Value × 0.0135) / 365`. This is deducted before the NAV is declared.
  • GA: A fixed % of the Annual Premium is converted into units at the current NAV and added to the fund.
