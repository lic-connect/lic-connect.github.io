Plan No. 752: The Story of Mr. Anand’s "Smart Wealth Pot"

Imagine you want a "Smart Wealth Pot" where almost every rupee you put in goes straight to work. This is LIC’s SIIP (Plan 752)—a Systemic Investment Insurance Plan. Here is how an agent explains it to Mr. Anand (Age 30), who invests ₹1,00,000 every year for 20 years:

  1. The Small Gate (PAC): Unlike other plans, SIIP has a very low entry fee. If PAC is 5% in Year 1, LIC only takes ₹5,000.
    Math: ₹1,00,000 - ₹5,000 = ₹95,000 invested in the market immediately.
  2. The Gold Coins (Units): If the market price (NAV) is ₹10, Mr. Anand gets his first batch of units.
    Math: ₹95,000 ÷ ₹10 = 9,500 Units credited.
  3. The Monthly Maintenance (Charges): To keep his ₹10,00,000 Life Cover active and manage his funds, LIC cancels a few units every month.
    Math: If charges are ₹200 and NAV is ₹15, LIC cancels 13.33 units.
  4. The Loyalty Reward (Guaranteed Additions): This is the highlight of SIIP! For staying invested, LIC adds "Free Bonus Units" into the pot at the end of years 6, 10, 15, 20, and 25.
    Math: In the 20th year, LIC might add 20% of his annual premium (₹20,000) as extra units!
  5. The Final Wealth: At the end of the term, Mr. Anand sells all his accumulated units (Original + GA) at the final NAV.

Key Technical Definitions

1. NAV (Net Asset Value): The current market price of one unit of your chosen fund. This changes daily based on market performance.

2. PAC (Premium Allocation Charge): The fee deducted from your premium. In SIIP, this is lower than traditional ULIPs to ensure more money is invested.

3. Admin Charges: A monthly fee for managing the policy records. In SIIP, these are deducted by cancelling units.

4. Mortality Charges: The cost of the insurance cover. It depends on your age and the difference between your Sum Assured and Fund Value.

5. FMC (Fund Management Charge): The 1.35% annual fee for the expertise of LIC fund managers, deducted daily from the NAV.

6. GA (Guaranteed Additions): Fixed percentages of your annual premium added to your unit fund at specific intervals (6, 10, 15, 20, 25 years).

How Each Charge is Calculated

  • PAC: Calculated as a percentage of your annual premium. (e.g., 5% in Yr 1, then reduces to 0% for online or low rates for offline).
  • Mortality Charge: `(Sum at Risk × Age Rate) / 12000`. This is deducted every month by cancelling units.
  • Admin Charge: A fixed monthly amount (e.g., ₹150) divided by the current NAV to determine the units to be deducted.
  • FMC: 1.35% per annum. It is calculated daily using the formula: `(Fund Value × 0.0135) / 365`. This deduction is baked into the NAV.
  • GA: Calculated as `Annual Premium × GA %`. This amount is converted to units at the NAV of that specific policy anniversary.
