Smart Pension

Plan No. 879

An Intelligent Annuity for Modern Needs.

Smart Pension (879) is a Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Individual/Group Immediate Annuity Single Premium Plan. The annuity payable is guaranteed at the inception of the policy.

Premium ModeSingle Premium (Lump Sum)
Annuity IntervalsMonthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Yearly
Min Entry Age18 Years (Completed)
Max Entry Age100 Yrs (Option F) / 70 Yrs (Option E3-E5)
Min Purchase Price₹1,00,000
Max Purchase PriceNo Upper Limit

Single Life Annuity Options

  • A: Life Annuity
  • B1 to B4: Annuity Certain for 5, 10, 15, or 20 years and life thereafter
  • C1 & C2: Life Annuity increasing at simple rate of 3% or 6% p.a.
  • D: Life Annuity with Return of Balance Purchase Price
  • E1 to E4: Life Annuity with 50% or 100% return of Purchase price after age 75 or 80
  • E5: Life Annuity with 5% return of Purchase price from age 76 to 95
  • F: Life Annuity with Return of Purchase Price

Joint Life Annuity Options

  • G1 & G2: Joint Life (50% or 100% annuity to secondary annuitant)
  • H1 to I2: Joint Life increasing at 3% or 6% p.a. (50% or 100% to secondary)
  • J: Joint Life with 100% annuity to last survivor and Return of Purchase Price

Death Benefit Options (D, E1-E5, F, J)

• Lump Sum: Full payment to the nominee.

• Annuitisation: Payout at prevailing immediate annuity rates.

• Instalments: Received in 5, 10, or 15 instalments.

Liquidity Option (Options F and J Only)

Withdraw a lump-sum amount by reducing annuity payments (After 5 years from DOC). Maximum 3 withdrawals allowed. Total withdrawals cannot exceed 60% of Purchase Price.

Loan FacilityAvailable for E1-E5, F, and J (after 3 months)
SurrenderGuaranteed at inception

Form No 444 with Suitability Analysis Form and Benefit Illustration shall be used.
