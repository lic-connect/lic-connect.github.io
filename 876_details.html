Core Benefits

Death Benefit

In case of unfortunate death of the Life Assured during the policy term, the "Sum Assured on Death" is payable to the nominee.

  • Regular/Limited Premium: Highest of 7x Annualized Premium, 105% of Premiums Paid, or Absolute Amount Assured.
  • Single Premium: Higher of 125% of Single Premium or Absolute Amount Assured.
  • Accident Benefit Rider: Optional cover available for accidental death.

Plan Highlights

This is a pure risk protection plan focused on high coverage at affordable rates.

  • Online Only: Can be purchased directly via LIC website.
  • Special Rates: Lower premium rates for women.
  • High SA Rebate: Attractive rebates for higher Sum Assured.
  • Maturity Benefit: Nil (This is a pure Term Assurance plan).

Flexibility in Cover: Two Options

Option 1 (Level Sum Assured): The Basic Sum Assured remains constant throughout the policy term.
Option 2 (Increasing Sum Assured): The Sum Assured increases by 10% annually from the 6th year until the 15th year (up to 2x Basic SA), offering protection against inflation.

Note: GST is not applicable. Loan facility is not available under this plan. For details, contact us.
