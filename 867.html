LIC New Pension Plus (Plan 867)

Plan No. 867: The Story of Mr. Suresh’s "Retirement Power-Bank"

Imagine you want to build a "Retirement Power-Bank" that stores energy (wealth) specifically for your old age. This is LIC’s New Pension Plus (Plan 867). Here is how an agent explains it to Mr. Suresh (Age 30), who invests ₹1,00,000 every year:

  1. The Entry Gate (PAC): Out of Suresh's ₹1,00,000, LIC takes a small processing fee. In Year 1, this is 7%.
    Math: ₹1,00,000 - ₹7,000 = ₹93,000 goes into his retirement fund.
  2. Buying the Retirement Coins (Units): If the NAV is ₹10, Suresh gets his first set of units.
    Math: ₹93,000 ÷ ₹10 = 9,300 Units.
  3. The "Zero-Waste" Advantage (No Mortality): Most ULIPs charge for life insurance. But here, there are NIL Mortality Charges. This means no units are cancelled for "insurance cover," allowing the power-bank to charge faster!
  4. The Retirement Booster (GA): To reward Suresh for staying consistent, LIC adds "Free Coins" (Guaranteed Additions) at Year 6, Year 10, and every single year from the 11th year onwards.
    Math: In Year 15, LIC adds 15.5% of his premium = ₹15,500 worth of free units added to his pot.
  5. The Golden Harvest (Vesting): When Suresh retires, he uses this massive pot to buy a "Pension Pipe" (Annuity) that pays him a monthly income for life.

Key Technical Definitions

1. NAV (Net Asset Value): The daily price of one unit of the Pension Fund. This tracks the performance of the stocks and bonds.

2. PAC (Premium Allocation Charge): The processing fee deducted from your premium. In Plan 867, it is 7% in Yr 1, 4% in Yrs 2-5, and 3% thereafter.

3. Admin Charges: A fixed monthly fee for maintaining your pension account, deducted by cancelling units.

4. FMC (Fund Management Charge): The 1.35% annual fee for LIC’s investment experts, adjusted daily within the NAV.

5. GA (Guaranteed Additions): Extra money added to your fund by LIC at specific intervals to boost your retirement corpus.

6. Assured Benefit: On death before retirement, LIC guarantees to pay 105% of all premiums paid or the Fund Value (whichever is higher).

How Charges are Calculated

  • PAC: Deducted from your premium before investment. (e.g., Year 1: 7%, Year 2-5: 4%, Year 6+: 3%).
  • Mortality Charge: NIL / Zero. This plan does not deduct any charges for life insurance cover.
  • Admin Charge: A fixed monthly fee (e.g., ₹100 per month) converted into units at the current NAV and cancelled from your fund.
  • FMC: 1.35% per year of the Fund Value. Calculated as: `(Fund Value × 0.0135) / 365` and deducted daily before the NAV is published.
  • GA: Calculated as a % of your Annual Premium. For example, at Year 6 it's 5%, at Year 10 it's 10%, and it increases every year thereafter.
