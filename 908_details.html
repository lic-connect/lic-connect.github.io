Decisive Staged Coverage

Two-Stage Financial Fortress

Designed to fund treatment aggressively at inception and deliver continuous income replacement during advanced stages.

Phase 01 🔬

Early Stage Cancer

Lump Sum Payout 25% of Applicable SA
  • Instant Liquidity: 25% of Applicable Sum Assured paid upon confirmed histological diagnosis.
  • 3-Year Premium Waiver: Premiums waived for the next 3 policy years (or balance term, whichever is lower) from next policy anniversary.
  • Major Stage Remains Active: Payout does not reduce subsequent Major Stage cover (100% remains fully payable).
  • Single Payout Limit: Payable once ever during policy duration for the first occurring event.
Covered Manifestations: Carcinoma-in-situ (CIS), Early Prostate (T1N0M0, Gleason 2-6), Thyroid (< 2.0cm, T1N0M0), Bladder (TaN0M0), Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia (Stage 0-2), Cervical Intraepithelial Neoplasia (CIN3).
Phase 02 🛡️

Major Stage Cancer

Lump Sum + Income 100% + 1% Monthly for 10 Yrs
  • 100% Lump Sum Benefit: Paid immediately irrespective of any earlier Early Stage claim already settled under the policy.
  • 10-Year Income Replacement: 1% of Applicable Sum Assured paid every month for 120 continuous months (totaling an extra 120% of SA).
  • Nominee Protection: Monthly income continues for the full 10-year term even if Life Assured dies or if the 10 years extend beyond maturity date.
  • Total Premium Cessation: All future policy premiums cease entirely from policy anniversary following diagnosis.
Definition: Malignant tumor characterized by uncontrolled growth and spread with invasion & destruction of normal tissues. Includes leukemia, lymphoma, and sarcoma confirmed by histology.

Level Sum Assured

The Sum Assured increases by 10% of Basic Sum Assured on each of the first five policy anniversaries, automatically combating medical inflation.

Can grow up to 150% of Basic Sum Assured by Year 6. Freezes permanently if cancer is diagnosed.

⚠️ Excluded Cancer Conditions

  • Tumors described as benign, pre-malignant, borderline malignant, or of low malignant potential.
  • Dysplasia, intra-epithelial neoplasia, or squamous intra-epithelial lesions (except specified CIN3).
  • Carcinoma in-situ of skin and melanoma in-situ.
  • Any non-melanoma skin carcinoma without evidence of metastases to lymph nodes.
  • Prostate cancers with Gleason score ≤ 6 (for Major Stage) or T1N0M0.
  • Thyroid cancers classified histologically as T1N0M0 or below (for Major Stage).
  • Gastro-Intestinal Stromal Tumors (GIST) classified as T1N0M0 with mitotic count ≤ 5/50 HPFs.

📑 General Exclusions & Revivals

  • Pre-Existing Conditions: Diagnosed or treated within 36 months prior to policy issuance or revival (Early stage claimed policies excused on revival).
  • Substance Abuse: Medical conditions caused directly or indirectly by alcohol or narcotics not prescribed by an RMP.
  • Nuclear Radiation: Nuclear fuel, explosive radioactivity, or contamination hazards.
  • 5-Year Revival Window: Lapsed policy can be revived within 5 consecutive years from first unpaid premium (Revival interest rate: 9.5% p.a. for 2026-27).
  • Pure Risk Nature: No Death Benefit, No Maturity Benefit, No Surrender Value, and No Loan Facility available under this plan.
