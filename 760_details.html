Benefit Structure

On Maturity

On survival to the end of the policy term, you receive:

  • Sum Assured on Maturity (Basic Sum Assured)
  • + Accrued Guaranteed Additions

On Death

Before Risk Commencement: Refund of premiums paid (excluding taxes/extras).

After Risk Commencement: Sum Assured on Death + Accrued Guaranteed Additions.

Sum Assured on Death is higher of:
1. 125% of Basic Sum Assured
2. 7 times Annualized Premium
3. 105% of Total Premiums Paid

Additional Details

Risk Commencement for Minors: Starts 2 years from commencement or at age 8 (whichever is earlier). For age ≥ 8, risk starts immediately.

Riders Available: Term Assurance, Critical Illness, Premium Waiver Benefit.

Loan & Surrender: Available after payment of at least 2 full years' premiums.
