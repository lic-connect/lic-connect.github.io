Saral Jeevan Bima

Plan No. 859

Pure Protection, Simplified.

SARAL JEEVAN BIMA (859) is a Standard, Non-Linked, Without Profits, Pure Risk Cover Plan. It is designed to provide high life insurance protection at an affordable cost, ensuring financial security for your loved ones.

Key Specifications

Policy Term5 to 40 Years
Entry Age18 to 65 Years (Last B'day)
Sum Assured₹5,00,000 to ₹25,00,000
Waiting Period45 Days from acceptance

Premium Options

Payment OptionsRegular, Limited, or Single
Payment ModesYearly, Half-Yearly, Monthly (NACH)

Death Benefit Structure

During 45-Day Waiting Period

Natural Death: Refund of 100% of all premiums paid (excluding taxes).

Accidental Death: Full Sum Assured on Death is payable.

After Waiting Period

Natural or Accidental Death: 100% of the Basic Sum Assured is payable to the nominee.

Other Benefits & Clauses

Maturity BenefitNothing is Payable
Survival BenefitNothing is Payable
Surrender ValueNo Surrender Value Available
Loan FacilityNo Loan Facility Available

Tax Benefit: Premiums paid are eligible for tax rebate under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

Proposal Forms 300 and 340 shall be used under this plan.
