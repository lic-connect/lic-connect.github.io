Jeevan Utsav

Jeevan Utsav (771) is a non-linked, guaranteed benefit, whole life assurance plan designed for lifelong financial security.

Plan 771

Plan at a Glance

Policy Term

100 - Age at Entry

Premium Term

5 to 16 Years

Entry Age

90 Days to 65 Years

Sum Assured

Min. ₹5,00,000 (No Max Limit)

The Utsav Guarantee: Additions + Income

Guaranteed additions @ ₹40 per 1000 Sum Assured accrue throughout the premium paying term. Survival benefits @ 10% of Sum Assured are available for life.

Benefit Structure

On Survival

  • Annual Income: 10% of Basic Sum Assured paid every year (from the specific income start year) till 100 years of age.
  • Maturity: On surviving till age 100, Sum Assured + Accrued Guaranteed Additions are paid.

On Death

Nominee receives WHICHEVER IS HIGHER of:

  • Basic Sum Assured + Accrued GA.
  • 7 times of Annualized Premium.
  • 105% of all Premiums paid as on date of death.

Operational Features

Premium Modes Yearly, Halfly, Quarterly, Monthly (ECS)
Loan Facility Available after 2 full years of payment
Surrender Value Available after 2 full years of payment
Income Tax (80C) Eligible for Tax Rebate
Income Tax (10/10D) Maturity is Tax Free
Rider Facility ADDB Rider available up to age 70

Proposal Forms: 300, 340, or 360 shall be used under this plan depending on the case.
