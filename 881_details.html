LIC’s Bima Lakshmi is a Non-Par, Non-Linked, Individual, Savings Plan designed exclusively for females. This limited premium payment plan provides life cover, savings, and survival benefits with Guaranteed Additions.

Policy TermFixed 25 Years
Premium Term7 to 15 Years
Entry Age18 (Min) to 50 (Max)
Min Sum Assured₹2,00,000 (No Max Limit)
Guaranteed Add.7% of Tabular Premium
Auto CoverAfter 3 Yrs Premiums

Survival Benefit Choices

Choose one option at the proposal stage (cannot be changed later):

Option A

Lump sum: 50% of Basic SA at the end of PPT

Option B

7.5% of Basic SA payable every 2nd year (Yr 2 to 24)

Option C

15% of Basic SA payable every 4th year (Yr 4 to 24)

Core Policy Benefits

  • Maturity Benefit: Sum Assured on Maturity (Equal to Basic Sum Assured) + Accrued Guaranteed Additions.
  • Death Benefit: Sum Assured on Death + Accrued Guaranteed Additions. Sum Assured on Death is higher of Basic SA or 10x Tabular Annual Premium. (Min 105% of premiums).
  • Deferment Option: You can defer Survival Benefits for 5 years to accumulate interest (Accumulation rate for 2025-26: 6.09% p.a.).
  • Settlement Option: Option to receive Maturity/Death benefit in installments over 5, 10, or 15 years.

Incentives & Liquidity

  • High Sum Assured: Rebates up to 0.50% on Guaranteed Addition rate for high SA (₹25L+).
  • Online Sale: Incentive up to 1.50% on Guaranteed Addition rate for direct purchases.
  • Existing Customer: Loyalty incentive for existing policyholders or nominees.
  • Loan Facility: Available after 1st year completion (provided 1 full year premium paid).

Optional Riders

  • Accidental Death & Disability Benefit Rider / Accident Benefit Rider
  • New Term Assurance Rider
  • Female Critical Illness Benefit Rider: Covers Modules for Cancer, Surgeries, and Pregnancy complications.

Guaranteed Additions accrue annually at 7% of Total Tabular Annual Premium throughout the policy term.
