Yuva Credit Life

Plan No. 877

Protect Your Loans, Secure Your Family.

YUVA CREDIT (877) is a Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Individual, Pure Risk Life Insurance Plan. This plan provides a robust safety net against loan repayment for the insured's family in the event of the life assured's death.

Plan TypeDecreasing Term Assurance
Entry Age18 to 45 Years (Last B'day)
Min. Sum Assured₹50,00,000
Max. Sum Assured₹5 Cr (Higher on case-to-case)
Premium ModesLimited or Single Premium
Payment FrequencyYearly, Half-Yearly

Policy Term & PPT Options

• Single Premium: Policy term of 5 to 30 years.

• Limited PPT (5 Yrs): Policy term of 10 to 30 years.

• Limited PPT (10 Yrs): Policy term of 15 to 30 years.

• Limited PPT (15 Yrs): Policy term of 25 to 30 years.

How Risk Cover Works

The risk cover reduces as per a pre-defined schedule based on the interest rate chosen by the proposer. You can choose from the following interest rates to match your loan:

Available Interest Rates 6%, 7%, 8%, 9%, 10%, 11% or 12%

Death Benefits

Limited Premium Policy: Sum Assured on Death is the highest of 105% of all premiums paid or the Absolute amount assured to be paid on death (as per schedule).

Single Premium Policy: Sum Assured on Death is the Absolute amount assured to be paid on death at the time of claim.

Other Important Details

Survival BenefitNothing is Payable
Loan FacilityNo Loan Granted
Tax BenefitEligible under Section 80C
Surrender ValuePayable for both Single & Ltd PPT

Proposal Form 300 with Annexure shall be used under this plan.
