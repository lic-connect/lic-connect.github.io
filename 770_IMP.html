Key Plan Conditions (Plan 770):
• RIB (Regular Income Benefit): 10% of Basic Sum Assured payable annually starting from end of PPT up to policy anniversary immediately prior to maturity.
• Booster Income: 70% of Basic Sum Assured paid once at designated policy anniversary (PPT 7: 12th yr | PPT 10: 15th yr | PPT 12: 17th yr | PPT 15: 20th yr | PPT 18: 23rd yr).
• Guaranteed Additions: Accrues strictly during the Premium Paying Term (End of Policy Years 1 to PPT) at the rate of base ₹70/1,000 AP + High BSA incentive + Existing Policyholder incentive.
