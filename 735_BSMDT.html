Plan No. 735: The Story of Mr. Rohan’s "Financial Tree"

Imagine you want to grow a "Financial Tree" that not only gives you wealth but also acts as an umbrella (Life Cover) for your family. This is how LIC’s New Endowment Plus (Plan 735) works for Mr. Rohan (Age 30), who invests ₹1,00,000 yearly:

  1. The Entry Fee (PAC): When Rohan pays ₹1,00,000, LIC takes a small "processing fee." In Year 1, this is 7.5%.
    Math: ₹1,00,000 - ₹7,500 (Fee) = ₹92,500 left to invest.
  2. Buying the Seeds (NAV): If the price of one "seed" (Unit) is ₹10, Rohan gets units in his account.
    Math: ₹92,500 ÷ ₹10 = 9,250 Units credited.
  3. The Umbrella & Maintenance (Charges): Every month, LIC provides a ₹10,00,000 Life Cover. Instead of asking for cash, they cancel a few units.
    Math: If monthly charges are ₹200 and NAV is ₹11, LIC removes approx. 18 units.
  4. The Market Growth: Over time, the value of each seed (NAV) grows based on market performance. If the NAV grows to ₹50 over 20 years...
  5. The Harvest (Maturity): At the end, Rohan gets the total units remaining multiplied by the latest NAV.
    Math: 15,000 Units × ₹50 = ₹7,50,000.

Technical Definitions

1. NAV (Net Asset Value): The daily price of one unit of the fund. It’s the "Market Price" of your investment.

2. PAC (Premium Allocation Charge): The upfront fee deducted from your premium before investment.

3. Admin Charges: A fixed fee deducted monthly for the administration and paperwork of your policy.

4. Mortality Charges: The fee for providing Life Insurance (the "Umbrella"). It depends on your age and sum at risk.

5. FMC (Fund Management Charge): The fee for the fund managers who trade in the market for you.

6. GA (Guaranteed Additions): In Plan 735, returns are market-linked; standard fixed additions are usually not applicable as in traditional plans.

How Charges are Calculated

  • PAC: Year 1: 7.5% | Year 2-5: 5% | Year 6+: 3%. (Deducted from Premium).
  • Mortality Charge: (Sum at Risk × Mortality Rate) / 12000. (Deducted Monthly via Units).
  • Admin Charge: Fixed amount (approx. ₹30-₹100/month) divided by NAV. (Deducted Monthly via Units).
  • FMC: 0.70% p.a. of the total fund value. (Adjusted daily in the NAV itself).
