How the Numbers Work: A Practical Example

Scenario: You pay a Premium of ₹50,000 in Year 1. The Initial NAV is ₹10.

Step 1: Allocation & Investment

First, LIC deducts the Fund Allocation Charge (FAC). For Year 1, this is 7.5%.

Charge = ₹50,000 × 7.5% = ₹3,750
Investable Amount = ₹50,000 - ₹3,750 = ₹46,250

Step 2: Buying Units

This money buys units at the current price (NAV).

Units Bought = ₹46,250 / ₹10 (NAV) = 4,625 Units

Step 3: Market Growth (NAV Increase)

Assuming an 8% growth rate, the Unit Price (NAV) increases by the end of the year.

New NAV = ₹10 + 8% = ₹10.80

Step 4: Deducting Other Charges

Charges for Mortality (Insurance cover), Admin (PAC), and Riders are totaled. Let's assume total charges are ₹1,200. These are paid by cancelling units at the New NAV.

Units Cancelled = ₹1,200 / ₹10.80 = 111.11 Units
Units Balance = 4,625 - 111.11 = 4,513.89 Units

Step 5: Final Fund Value

Your Fund Value is simply your remaining units multiplied by the current price.

Fund Value = 4,513.89 Units × ₹10.80 = ₹48,750

Death Benefit Calculation Example:
If death occurs, the nominee receives the HIGHEST of 3 values. Using the example above:

  • a) Basic Sum Assured (10x Premium): ₹5,00,000
  • b) Fund Value: ₹48,750
  • c) 105% of Premiums Paid: ₹52,500
Death Benefit Payable = ₹5,00,000
