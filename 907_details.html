7 Robust Benefit Pillars

Benefits are benchmarked against the Applicable Daily Benefit (ADB) chosen between ₹2,500 and ₹10,000, payable irrespective of actual hospital bills.

🏥
Daily Cash

Hospital Cash Benefit (HCB)

General Ward: 1x ADB | ICU: 2x ADB
  • Payable for continuous stay exceeding 24 hours (subsequent parts > 4 hrs covered).
  • Combined stay ICU > 4 hrs pays full 24-hr day at double (ICU) rate.
  • Hemodialysis & Radiotherapy covered even if hospital stay is < 24 hrs.
Year 1: 30 Days Year 2+: 90 Days/yr Lifetime: 900 Days
🔬
Primary Pillar

Major Surgical Benefit (MSB)

MSB Sum Assured = 100 x ADB
  • Lump sum % of MSB SA based on surgical procedure category.
  • Ambulance Bonus: Extra ₹1,000 lump sum per surgery.
  • 1-Year Premium Waiver: For Category 1 or 2 surgeries.
  • MSB Restoration: Extra coverage if 100% SA exhausted in a year for different surgical categories.
Annual: 100% MSB SA Lifetime: 10x MSB SA
⚡
Day Care

Day Care Procedure (DCPB)

Fixed Lump Sum = 5 x ADB
  • Specified specialised surgical procedures completed within 24 hours.
  • Paid regardless of actual costs incurred in hospital/day-care center.
  • Covers high-frequency advanced medical interventions.
Annual Cap: 3 Procedures Lifetime: 30 Procedures
🩺
Other Surgeries

Other Surgical Benefit (OSB)

Daily Allowance = 2.5 x ADB / day
  • Covers surgeries not categorized under MSB or DCPB.
  • Requires continuous hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours.
  • HCB is paid over and above OSB for length of hospital stay.
Yr 1: 15 Days | Yr 2+: 45 D/yr Lifetime: 450 Days
🛡️
Infections

Medical Management (MMB)

Lump Sum = 2.5 x ADB
  • Inpatient treatment for 5 listed critical infections:
  • Dengue • Malaria • Pneumonia • Pulmonary TB • Viral Hepatitis A
  • Hospital Cash Benefit paid in addition based on hospital stay.
Annual Cap: 2 Times Lifetime: 20 Times
⏳
Prolonged Stay

Extended Hospitalisation

Lump Sum = 10 x ADB
  • Triggered on a single continuous inpatient hospitalisation exceeding 30 days.
  • Payable in addition to HCB, MSB, OSB, and MMB.
  • Provides high-liquidity financial cushion during prolonged recovery.
Annual Cap: 1 Time Lifetime: 10 Times
❤️
Wellness

Health Check-Up Benefit

Reimbursement: Up to 0.5 x ADB
  • Available after every 3 consecutive policy years from commencement.
  • Reimburses actual check-up bills up to 50% of prevailing ADB.
  • Encourages preventive wellness and routine diagnostics for all members.
Frequency: Once Every 3 Years Applies: Per Insured

Infinite Growth

No Claim Benefit (NCB)

Every block of 3 consecutive claim-free policy years adds an extra 10% of Initial Daily Benefit (IDB) to the Applicable Daily Benefit.

🚀 Uncapped Accumulation — No lifetime maximum limit on total NCB accrual!
Family Security

Auto Health Cover (AHC)

Upon unfortunate death of the original Principal Insured, all future base premiums are 100% WAIVED for all surviving members for the remainder of the term.

🛡️ Seamless continuity under eldest major survivor as new PI.
Instant Relief

Quick Cash Facility

Undergo Category 1 or 2 Major Surgeries in any listed network hospital and receive 50% advance payout of the eligible MSB claim during hospitalisation itself.

⚡ Zero need to wait for post-discharge claim settlement.
