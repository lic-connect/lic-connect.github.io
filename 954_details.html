Key Benefits

Death Benefit

In case of unfortunate death of the Life Assured during the policy term, the "Sum Assured on Death" is payable.

  • Regular/Limited Premium: Highest of 7x Annualized Premium, 105% of Premiums Paid, or Absolute Amount Assured.
  • Single Premium: Higher of 125% of Single Premium or Absolute Amount Assured.
  • Accident Benefit Rider: Optional extra protection for accidental death.

Plan Features

Designed for the digital age with flexible options and competitive rates.

  • Online Exclusive: Available only through LIC website.
  • Special Rates: Lower premium rates for women and non-smokers.
  • Installment Option: Death benefit can be received in installments (5/10/15 years).
  • Maturity Benefit: Nil (Pure protection plan).

Flexibility in Cover: Level vs Increasing

Option 1 (Level Sum Assured): The Basic Sum Assured remains constant throughout the policy term.
Option 2 (Increasing Sum Assured): The Sum Assured increases by 10% annually from the 6th year until the 15th year (capped at 2x Basic SA), combating inflation.
