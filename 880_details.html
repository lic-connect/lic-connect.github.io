Jan Suraksha

Plan No. 880

UIN: 512N388V01

Chhota Premium, Badi Suraksha.

LIC’s Jan Suraksha is a Non-Par, Non-linked, Individual, Savings, Life Micro Insurance plan which offers a combination of protection and savings. It provides financial support for the family and a guaranteed lump sum at maturity.

Sum Assured₹1,00,000 - ₹2,00,000
Policy Term12 to 20 Years
Premium Paying TermPolicy Term minus 5 Yrs
Min/Max Entry Age18 - 55 Years

Eligibility & Restrictions

  • Basic Sum Assured Multiples: ₹5,000
  • Maximum Maturity Age: 70 years (65 years for POSP-LI/CPSC-SPV)
  • Medical Examination: Not required (available for standard healthy lives)
  • Maximum Age at Entry (POSP-LI): 53 years

Policy Benefits

  • Maturity Benefit: Sum Assured on Maturity (Equal to BSA) + Accrued Guaranteed Additions.
  • Death Benefit: Higher of 7 times Annualized Premium or Basic Sum Assured, plus accrued Guaranteed Additions. Minimum 105% of total premiums paid.
  • Guaranteed Additions: 4.00% of Total Annualized Premiums accrue at the end of each policy year.

Incentives & Riders

  • High Sum Assured Incentive: 0.25% increase in GA rate for BSA of ₹2,00,000.
  • Online Sale Incentive: 1.00% increase in GA rate for direct online purchases.
  • Optional Riders: Accidental Death & Disability Benefit Rider OR Accident Benefit Rider (available on additional premium).

Smart Protection Features

Auto Cover Period:
• After 3-5 years paid: 6 months of cover.
• After 5+ years paid: 2 years of cover.

Loan Facility: Available after payment of 1 full year's premium and completion of the first policy year (Up to 80% for in-force, 70% for paid-up).

Grace Period: 30 days for Y/H/Q modes and 15 days for Monthly mode.

Non-Par, Non-Linked, Individual, Savings, Life Micro Insurance Plan.

Premium Payment Modes: Yearly, Half-yearly, Quarterly, Monthly (NACH) or Salary deductions.
