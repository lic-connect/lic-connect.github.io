What does this ratio mean?
This ratio shows how efficiently each rupee of premium converts into maturity value.

  • Lower Ratio (≈ 2.11) → Indicates a protection-heavy plan. A significant part of the premium pays for lifelong insurance cover, not just returns.
  • Higher Ratio (≈ 3.85) → Indicates a return-efficient plan. More of your money works purely towards wealth creation.
  • Balanced–Strong Ratio (≈ 2.78) → Indicates a well-optimised combined strategy. This ratio reflects a thoughtful balance where part of the premium secures lifelong insurance protection, while the remaining portion works efficiently toward long-term wealth creation.

✔ Which one is better?
Neither alone. The real power comes from combining both — one plan protects your family for life, the other aggressively builds wealth.

Why this combination works:
The customer receives a sizeable payout at Year 15 to handle mid-life goals, while another corpus is built till Year 20. Even after enjoying maturity benefits, the Jeevan Anand policy continues to protect the family for life.
