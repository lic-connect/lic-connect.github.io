Mathematical Verification & Glossary

1. Premium Allocation Charge (PAC): 3.3% is deducted upfront. Only 96.7% is used to buy units.

2. NAV Spread: The NAV grows at the selected growth rate compounded annually.
Formula: $NAV_{Year} = 10 \times (1 + r)^{(Year-1)}$

3. Guaranteed Additions (GA): Loyalty additions are added to the unit fund at the end of specific years (6, 10, 15, 20, 25).
Year 6: 3% | Yr 10: 4% | Yr 15: 5% | Yr 20: 6% | Yr 25: 7% (of Single Premium).

4. Death Benefit: In Plan 749, the nominee gets the Higher of the Basic Sum Assured or the Unit Fund Value.
