Plan No. 749: The Story of Mr. Ajay’s "Golden One-Time Seed"

Imagine you have a lump sum of money and you want to plant a "Golden Seed" that grows into a massive tree without you having to water it every year. This is LIC’s Nivesh Plus (Plan 749)—a Single Premium plan. Here is how it works for Mr. Ajay (Age 35), who invests ₹5,00,000 just once:

  1. The Entry Toll (PAC): Since Ajay is only paying once, LIC takes a one-time "Setup Fee." If the PAC is 3.3%, LIC takes ₹16,500 immediately.
    Math: ₹5,00,000 - ₹16,500 = ₹4,83,500 actually goes into the market.
  2. Buying the Units (NAV): If the market price (NAV) today is ₹10, Ajay’s money buys "Units."
    Math: ₹4,83,500 ÷ ₹10 = 48,350 Units credited to his account.
  3. The Monthly Gardener (Charges): Even though Ajay doesn't pay more premiums, LIC still provides him with a ₹6,25,000 Life Cover (Option 1). Every month, LIC "plucks" a few units to pay for this cover.
    Math: If monthly charges are ₹150 and NAV is ₹15, LIC cancels 10 units from Ajay's pot.
  4. The Bonus Fruit (Guaranteed Additions): This is the best part! At specific years (6th, 10th, 15th, 20th, and 25th), LIC adds extra money into the pot just for staying invested.
    Math: At year 10, if the GA is 4%, LIC adds ₹20,000 worth of extra units to Ajay's pot for free.
  5. The Mega Harvest (Maturity): After 20 years, Ajay takes all his remaining units (including the bonus ones) and sells them at the then-current market price (NAV).

Key Technical Definitions

1. NAV (Net Asset Value): The "Daily Price" of one unit of your fund. Your wealth = Total Units × Current NAV.

2. PAC (Premium Allocation Charge): The one-time fee deducted from your Single Premium before the money is invested.

3. Admin Charges: A fixed monthly administrative fee deducted by cancelling units (currently ₹0 for this plan in many cases, but kept as a provision).

4. Mortality Charges: The cost of the Life Cover. It is calculated monthly based on the "Sum at Risk."

5. FMC (Fund Management Charge): The fee (1.35% p.a.) charged by LIC experts for managing your investments in the stock/bond market.

6. GA (Guaranteed Additions): Fixed percentage of the Single Premium added to the unit fund at the end of specific policy years.

How Each Charge is Calculated

  • PAC: Deducted once at the start. For Offline: 3.30%, For Online: 1.50%.
  • Mortality Charge: (Sum at Risk × Mortality Rate for Age) / 12. Sum at Risk is (Sum Assured - Fund Value). As your fund grows, this charge usually decreases.
  • Admin Charge: In Plan 749, this is typically nil for the current duration, but generally fixed at a monthly rate if applicable.
  • FMC: 1.35% per annum of the fund value, deducted daily from the NAV calculation (before the price is shown to you).
  • GA Calculation: A fixed % (3% to 7%) of the Single Premium is converted into units and added to your balance at years 6, 10, 15, 20, and 25.
