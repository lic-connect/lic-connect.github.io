Saral Pension

Plan No. 862

A Simple Pension for a Worry-Free Life.

Saral Pension (862) is a Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Single Premium Individual Immediate Annuity Plan. It is designed to provide guaranteed financial independence through a straightforward income stream.

Premium ModeSingle Premium (Lump Sum)
Annuity IntervalsMonthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Yearly
Min Entry Age40 Years (Completed)
Max Entry Age80 Years (Last Birthday)
Min Purchase PriceBased on Minimum Annuity
Max Purchase PriceNo Upper Limit

Annuity Options

Option (i): Life Annuity with Return of 100% of Purchase Price.

Option (ii): Joint Life Last Survivor Annuity with Return of 100% of Purchase Price on death of the last survivor (Spouse only).

Policy Benefits

Benefit on Death

Under Option (i): Annuity payment ceases immediately and 100% of the Purchase Price is payable to Nominees/Legal Heirs.

Under Option (ii):
• On first death: 100% of the annuity continues as long as one Annuitant is alive.
• On death of last survivor: Annuity ceases and 100% of Purchase Price is paid to Nominees/Legal Heirs.
