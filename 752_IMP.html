LIC SIIP – Plan 752 (IRR Report)

Technical Insight: Net Yield (IRR)

The Net Yield (IRR) represents the actual interest rate earned after all expenses (Allocation, Mortality, Admin, and FMC). In the early years of SIIP, the IRR is usually negative because the upfront allocation charges eat into the capital. As the policy matures, the compounding and Guaranteed Additions pull the IRR higher.
