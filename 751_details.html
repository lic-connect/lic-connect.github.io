Micro Bachat

Plan No. 751
Small Savings. Big Security.

A Non-Linked, Participating Micro-Insurance Plan.
Designed specifically for Low-Income Groups, this plan offers a dual benefit of protection and savings with affordable premiums.

Insurance + Savings Liquidity

Key Benefits

Death Benefit

On unfortunate death during the policy term, the nominee receives the highest of:

  • Basic Sum Assured
  • 7 times Annualized Premium
  • 105% of Premiums Paid

+ Loyalty Addition (if death after 5 years).

Maturity Benefit

On survival to the end of the policy term, you receive:

Basic Sum Assured + Loyalty Addition (if any).

A lump sum amount to support your future financial goals.

Liquidity (Loan)

Available after payment of premiums for at least 3 full years.

  • In-force Policy: Up to 70% of Surrender Value
  • Paid-up Policy: Up to 60% of Surrender Value

Surrender Value

You can surrender the policy anytime after paying one full year's premium.

The Guaranteed Surrender Value depends on the policy term and year of surrender.

Additional Features

  • Rider: Accidental Death & Disability Benefit Rider available (up to Age 70).
  • Tax Benefit: Premiums eligible under Sec 80C. Maturity tax-free under Sec 10(10D).
  • Auto Cover: Extended risk cover period available if premiums are missed (after certain years).
