Plan No. 886: The Story of Mr. Vikram’s "Wealth Fortress"

Imagine you are building a "Wealth Fortress". You add bricks (money) every year, but you also want a guard (Insurance) to protect it. This is LIC’s Wealth Plus (Plan 886).

  1. Construction Fee: LIC takes a fee (PAC) before investing.
  2. Buying Units: Your money is converted into units at current NAV.
  3. Risk Cover: LIC provides a Life Cover by cancelling units monthly.
  4. Loyalty Bonus: Guaranteed Additions added at milestones.
Taxation Note: If total annual premium exceeds ₹2.5 Lakh, gains are taxed as LTCG (12.5% on profits exceeding ₹1.25L).
