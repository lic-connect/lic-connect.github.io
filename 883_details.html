The Power of Guaranteed Additions

During the chosen Guaranteed Addition Period (7–17 years), your policy grows with ₹40 per thousand Basic Sum Assured added every single year. This growth is guaranteed and fixed regardless of market conditions.

Comprehensive Benefits

Survival Benefits

Once the Guaranteed Addition Period ends, you can choose between two lifelong income options:

  • Option I (Regular Income): Receive a fixed payout of 10% of the Basic Sum Assured every year.
  • Option II (Flexi Income): Defer your 10% payout and let it accumulate at 5.5% p.a. interest, with the flexibility to withdraw up to 75% of the accumulated balance anytime.

Risk Cover & Maturity

  • On Death: Higher of (Basic Sum Assured) OR (1.25 x Tabular Single Premium) + all accrued Guaranteed Additions is paid to the nominee.
  • On Maturity: If the Life Assured survives to age 100, the Sum Assured on Maturity plus accrued GA is paid out.
  • Liquidity: Loan facility is available anytime after 3 months from policy issuance.

High Sum Assured Rebate: Significant premium discounts available for Basic Sum Assured of ₹10,00,000 and above.
