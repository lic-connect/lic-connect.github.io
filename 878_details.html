Core Benefits

Death Benefit

In case of unfortunate death of the Life Assured during the policy term, the "Sum Assured on Death" is payable, based on the Risk Cover Schedule.

  • Limited Premium: Higher of 105% of Total Premiums Paid or Absolute Amount Assured.
  • Single Premium: Absolute Amount Assured as per schedule.
  • Accident Benefit Rider: Optional cover available for accidental death (with Limited Premium).

Plan Highlights

A specialized protection plan that reduces coverage as your liability decreases.

  • Online Only: Exclusive online purchase via LIC website.
  • Special Rates: Lower premium rates for women.
  • High SA Rebate: Discounts available for higher coverage amounts.
  • Maturity Benefit: Nil (Pure protection plan).

Decreasing Risk Cover

The Death Benefit reduces over the term of the policy. You choose an interest rate (from 6% to 12%) at inception, and a Risk Cover Schedule is prepared. This ensures the insurance cover aligns with your outstanding loan balance.
