Policy Benefits

On Maturity: Guaranteed "Basic Sum Assured".

On Death (After Risk Commencement): "Sum Assured on Death", which is the higher of:

  • Basic Sum Assured
  • 7 times Annualized Premium

(Death Benefit shall not be less than 105% of Total Premiums Paid).

On Death (Before Risk Commencement): Refund of premiums paid (excluding taxes).

Loan & Surrender: Available after payment of at least 2 full years' premiums.
