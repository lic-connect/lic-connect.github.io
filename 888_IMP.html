Key Joint Life Rules (Plan 888 - Jeevan Sathi Single Premium):
• 1st Death: Sum Assured on Death is paid immediately to the surviving spouse. The policy continues on the life of the survivor without any further premium payment.
• 2nd Death: Sum Assured on Death plus full accrued Guaranteed Additions up to the year of death is paid, and the policy terminates.
• Simultaneous Death of Both: Sum of 1st and 2nd death benefits (Twice Sum Assured on Death + accrued GA) is paid to nominees.
• Maturity: On survival of at least one life to the end of the policy term, Basic Sum Assured + Total Accrued GA is paid.
• Net Cashflow: Calculated as Total Outflow minus Total Inflow (Negative indicates net out-of-pocket, positive indicates net return to policyholder).
