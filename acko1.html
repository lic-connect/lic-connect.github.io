Flexi Term Details

1. Plan Type Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Individual Pure Risk Premium Life Insurance.
2. Sum Assured (SA) Min: ₹50 Lakhs
Max: No Limit (Subject to Board Approved Underwriting Policy).
3. Policy Term Min: 10 Years
Max: To Age 85.
4. Premium Payment Term (PPT) Regular Pay, Limited Pay (5/10/15/etc), or Single Pay.
5. Entry Age Min: 18 Years
Max: 65 Years.
6. Options Available Life Protect (Standard), Life Protect Plus (Allows adding riders/benefits).
7. Maturity Benefit Nil (Unless Return of Premium variant is specifically chosen).
8. Survival Benefit (SB) Nil.
9. Death Benefit Sum Assured paid as Lump Sum immediately upon death of Life Assured.
10. Riders Acko Life Accidental Death Benefit Rider, Acko Life Critical Illness Rider.
11. Surrender Allowed. Surrender Value depends on PPT (usually available for Single/Limited pay after lock-in).
12. Revival Allowed within 5 consecutive years from the date of first unpaid premium.
USP: Who Should Buy? Digital-native breadwinners looking for high coverage at low cost. Offers flexibility to change coverage as life stages change.

Saral Jeevan Details

1. Plan Type Standard Individual Term Life Insurance (IRDAI Mandated).
2. Sum Assured (SA) Min: ₹5 Lakhs
Max: ₹25 Lakhs.
3. Policy Term Min: 5 Years
Max: 40 Years (Up to Age 70).
4. Premium Payment Term (PPT) Single Pay, Regular Pay, Limited Pay (5 or 10 years).
5. Entry Age Min: 18 Years
Max: 65 Years.
6. Options Available Standard plan structure across all insurers.
7. Maturity Benefit Nil. (Pure Risk Plan).
8. Survival Benefit (SB) Nil.
9. Death Benefit Highest of: 10x Annual Premium, 105% of all premiums paid, or Absolute SA.
10. Riders Accidental Death Benefit Rider and Permanent Disability Benefit Rider allowed.
11. Surrender Nil for Regular Pay. Allowed for Single/Limited Pay (formula based).
12. Revival Allowed within 5 years from date of first unpaid premium.
USP: Who Should Buy? First-time buyers seeking simple, standardized terms without hidden clauses. Suitable for lower sum assured needs.
