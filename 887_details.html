BIMA KAVACH

PLAN NO: 887 | UIN: 512N360V01

LIC’s Bima Kavach is a Non-Par, Non-Linked, Life, Individual, Pure Risk Plan that provides financial protection to the insured’s family in case of his/her unfortunate death during the policy term.

Eligibility Conditions

Min Entry Age: 18 Years (Last Birthday)
Max Entry Age: 65 Years (Last Birthday)
Min Maturity Age: 28 Years (Last Birthday)
Max Maturity Age: 100 Years (Last Birthday)
Min Sum Assured: ₹ 2,00,00,000 (2 Crore)
Max Sum Assured: No Limit

Death Benefit Options

Option I: Level Sum Assured
The Sum Assured remains constant throughout the policy term.

Option II: Increasing Sum Assured
Remains equal to Basic Sum Assured for the first 5 years. From the 6th to the 15th year, it increases by 10% of Basic Sum Assured every year until it doubles (2X). From the 16th year onwards, it remains constant at 2X until the end of the term.

Premium Payment Modes

Payment Type Minimum Policy Term Maximum Policy Term
Single Premium 10 Years 82 Years
Limited Pay (5/10/15 Yrs) 10 / 15 / 20 Years 82 Years
Regular Premium 10 Years 82 Years

Special Features

  • Life Stage Option: Option to increase cover on Marriage (50%) or Birth of Child (25% each) without medicals (Only for Level Sum Assured & Regular Pay).
  • High Sum Assured Rebate: Attractive discounts on premiums for larger Sum Assured.
  • Online Discount:
    • 7.50% Rebate on Tabular Premium for Regular/Limited Pay.
    • 3.00% Rebate on Tabular Premium for Single Pay.
  • Special Rates: Lower premium rates for Non-Smokers and Women.

Available Riders

LIC’s Accident Benefit Rider: Available under Regular and Limited Premium modes by paying additional premium.

Maturity & Surrender

  • Maturity Benefit: On survival to the end of the term, No Maturity Benefit is payable (Pure Risk Plan).
  • Surrender Value: No Surrender Value. However, Unexpired Risk Premium Value may be payable in specific cases for Single and Limited Premium policies.
