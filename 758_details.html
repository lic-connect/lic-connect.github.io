Plan 758

Single Premium, Deferred Annuity Plan

Product Overview

A non-linked, non-participating annuity plan where you pay once and enjoy guaranteed pension after a deferment period. Available for individuals including third gender.

Entry Age30 - 79 Years
Deferment Period1 to 12 Years
Min Purchase₹ 1,50,000
Max PurchaseNo Limit

Annuity Options

1. Deferred Annuity for Single Life

2. Deferred Annuity for Joint Life

*Joint Life includes Close Relatives: Grandparents, Parents, Spouse, Children, Grandchildren, or Siblings.

Key Features

  • Annuity Modes: Yearly, Half-yearly, Quarterly, Monthly.
  • Surrender: Allowed anytime after 3 months from policy completion.
  • Loan Facility: Available after 3 months from completion or free-look period expiry.
