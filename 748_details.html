Guaranteed Additions

Accrued at the end of each policy year:

  • First 5 Years: ₹ 50 per ₹ 1,000 Sum Assured
  • 6th Year to End of PPT: ₹ 55 per ₹ 1,000 Sum Assured

Survival Benefits (Money Back)

Fixed percentage of Basic Sum Assured (BSA) payable on survival to specific durations.

14 Year Term

30% of BSA in 10th & 12th year.

16 Year Term

35% of BSA in 12th & 14th year.

18 Year Term

40% of BSA in 14th & 16th year.

20 Year Term

45% of BSA in 16th & 18th year.

24 Year Term

45% of BSA in 20th & 22nd year.

28 Year Term

45% of BSA in 24th & 26th year.

Death Benefit Coverage

Sum Assured on Death is defined as the highest of:

  • 125% of Basic Sum Assured
  • 7 times Annualized Premium
  • 105% of all Premiums Paid

Payout Logic:

  • Death during first 5 years: SA on Death + Guaranteed Additions.
  • Death after 5 years: SA on Death + Guaranteed Additions + Loyalty Addition (LA).

Available Riders: Critical Illness Benefit & Premium Waiver Benefit.
