Dynamic Financial Controls

4 Strategic Liquidity Powers

Unprecedented flexibility engineered to respond to evolving life priorities during both deferment and retirement.

📈

Top-Up Annuity Facility

Accelerate your future income stream anytime during the deferment period by injecting additional single premiums (provided outstanding DP is ≥ 1 year).

  • Top-up annuity rates derived dynamically to harmonize with base vesting date.
  • Calculated independently with own high-premium incentives.
  • Total annuity payable equals Base Annuity + all Top-Up tranches combined.
💧

Liquidity Option (Cash In Hand)

Available under ROP Options (2, 9, 10, 11). Withdraw lump-sum cash in return for proportionate reduction in ongoing annuity payments and death benefit.

  • Exercisable after 5 years from first annuity payment.
  • Permitted up to 3 times during the currency of the policy.
  • Cumulative withdrawals allowed up to 60% of Total Premiums Paid.
⚡

Advanced Annuity on 1st Death

Under Joint Life ROP Options (9 & 11), on the unfortunate first death of either annuitant, the survivor can withdraw up to 5 years of annuity upfront.

  • Provides immediate discounted lump-sum cash value within 6 months of death.
  • Discount rate capped at 6.78% p.a. compounding half-yearly (2026-27).
  • Original annuity automatically resumes once the 5-year advance window concludes.
🛡️

Flexible Death Claim Settlement

Annuitant can predetermine how nominees receive the death benefit across three versatile avenues:

  • Lump-Sum: 100% paid out immediately in one consolidated settlement.
  • Annuitisation (+2% Bonus): Converted to Immediate Annuity with a +2.00% tabular incentive per ₹1,000 Purchase Price.
  • In Installments: Disbursed over 5, 10, or 15 years (interest rate: 5.11% p.a. for 2026-27).

♿ Divyangjan Dependent Benefit

Option 10 can be purchased for the benefit of a dependent with benchmark disability (min ₹50,000 Purchase Price). On demise of proposer, proceeds fund an immediate annuity for the Divyangjan with an additional +2% increase in tabular annuity rates.

🇬🇧 QROPS Compliant (UK Transfer)

Options 10 and 11 can be purchased as a Qualifying Recognized Overseas Pension Scheme (QROPS) through transfer of UK tax-relieved pension funds, subject to HMRC terms (min vesting age 55).

🔄 Surrender Value Assurance

Single premium policies can be surrendered at any time. Regular premium policies can be surrendered after paying at least 1 full year's premium (GSV equals 90% of premiums minus annuities paid post deferment).
