Plan No. 873: The Story of Mrs. Kavita’s "Index-Linked Jet"

Imagine you want to travel to wealth, and you decide to hitch a ride on a "Jet" that follows the top companies of India. This is LIC’s Index Plus (Plan 873)—a ULIP that specifically invests in the stock market indices. Here is how an agent explains it to Mrs. Kavita (Age 35), who invests ₹1,00,000 every year:

  1. The Boarding Pass (PAC): Out of Kavita's ₹1,00,000, LIC takes an entry fee. For an offline policy in Year 1, this is 7.5%.
    Math: ₹1,00,000 - ₹7,500 = ₹92,500 is used to buy "fuel" (units).
  2. Choosing the Flight Path (NAV): Kavita chooses the Flexi Growth Fund, which follows the Nifty 50 Index. If the NAV is ₹10, she gets her units.
    Math: ₹92,500 ÷ ₹10 = 9,250 Units.
  3. The Safety Belt & Pilot (Charges): LIC provides her with a ₹10,00,000 Life Cover. Every month, they cancel a few units to pay for this protection (Mortality Charge) and the management of the fund.
    Math: If monthly charges are ₹200 and NAV is ₹12, LIC cancels 16.6 units.
  4. Riding the Index: Because her fund follows the Nifty 50, if India's top 50 companies grow by 12% in a year, the price of her units (NAV) aims to mirror that growth.
  5. The Loyalty Fuel (GA): At the end of Year 6, Year 10, and every 5 years after, LIC adds Guaranteed Additions (Free units) to her fund just for staying on the flight.
    Math: At year 15, LIC adds 15% of premium = ₹15,000 worth of extra units.

Key Technical Definitions

1. NAV (Net Asset Value): The price of one unit of your index fund. It changes daily based on how the Nifty 50 or Nifty Next 50 performs.

2. PAC (Premium Allocation Charge): The fee deducted from your premium. It is higher in Year 1 (7.5% offline) and drops significantly in later years.

3. Admin Charges: A monthly administrative fee deducted by cancelling units from your fund. Currently, it is ₹0 for many online modes but applies to offline.

4. Mortality Charges: The cost of providing the Life Cover. It is deducted monthly and reduces as your fund value grows closer to the Sum Assured.

5. FMC (Fund Management Charge): The 1.35% annual fee for managing the index fund, deducted daily from the NAV.

6. GA (Guaranteed Additions): Fixed percentages of the annual premium added as units at the end of the 6th, 10th, 15th, 20th, and 25th years.

How Each Charge is Calculated

  • PAC: Calculated as a % of your premium. Offline: Yr 1 (7.5%), Yr 2-5 (5%), Yr 6+ (3%). Online: Yr 1 (3%), Yr 2-5 (2%), Yr 6+ (1.5%).
  • Mortality Charge: `(Sum at Risk × Age Rate) / 12000`. Sum at Risk = (Sum Assured - Fund Value).
  • Admin Charge: A fixed monthly amount divided by the current NAV to determine the units to be cancelled.
  • FMC: 1.35% per year, deducted daily from the NAV calculation.
  • GA Calculation: `Annual Premium × GA %`. This amount is added to the unit fund on the policy anniversary.
