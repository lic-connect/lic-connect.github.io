Death Benefits

Before Commencement of Risk

An amount equal to the total amount of premiums paid (excluding taxes, extra premium and rider premium, if any) shall be payable.

After Commencement of Risk

"Sum Assured on Death" + Vested Bonuses + FAB (if any).

Sum Assured on Death is higher of:
1. 125% of Basic Sum Assured
2. 7 times of Annualized Premium

*Death benefit shall not be less than 105% of total premiums paid.

Other Key Features

Liquidity (Loan)

Loan facility is available after payment of premiums for at least 2 full years.

Surrender

Policy can be surrendered anytime after payment of 2 full years' premiums.

Tax Benefits

Premium: Rebate under Sec 80C.
Maturity/Death Benefit: Tax-free under Sec 10(10D).
