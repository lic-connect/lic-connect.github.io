Key Plan Conditions (Plan 890 - New Bima Jyoti):
• Guaranteed Additions: Accrues at the end of each policy year throughout the entire policy term (even after PPT) at 6% of Total Annualized Premiums in respect of premiums paid, plus High BSA and Online Sale incentives.
• Death Benefit: Higher of 125% of Basic Sum Assured or 7 times Annualized Premium, plus cumulative accrued GA. Minimum 105% of total premiums paid to date. (For age < 8, risk commences after 2 years or attainment of age 8).
• Net Cashflow: Total Outflow minus Total Inflow (Positive represents net outflow / premium paid; negative represents net inflow / maturity benefit received).
