1. Guaranteed Additions (GA)

Growing Returns

Guaranteed Additions accrue at the end of each policy year per ₹1,000 Basic Sum Assured (BSA). The rate increases as your policy gets older:

Policy Year Guaranteed Addition (per ₹1000 BSA)
1st to 5th Year ₹ 50
6th to 10th Year ₹ 55
11th to 25th Year ₹ 60

2. Survival & Maturity Schedule

Policy Term (Years) Premium Paying Term Survival Benefit (Money Back) Maturity Benefit
15 Years 11 Years 25% of BSA at end of 13th & 14th Year 50% of BSA + Accrued GA
20 Years 16 Years 25% of BSA at end of 18th & 19th Year 50% of BSA + Accrued GA
25 Years 21 Years 25% of BSA at end of 23rd & 24th Year 50% of BSA + Accrued GA

3. Death Benefit Coverage

Sum Assured on Death

Higher of:

  • 125% of Basic Sum Assured
  • 7 times of Annualized Premium

Plus Accrued Guaranteed Additions.

(Total Death Benefit shall not be less than 105% of total premiums paid).

For Minor Lives

Entry Age < 8 Years: Risk starts 2 years after commencement or at age 8 (whichever is earlier). Death benefit before risk commencement is refund of premiums.

Entry Age ≥ 8 Years: Immediate risk cover.

Settlement Options

You can choose to receive Maturity Benefit or Death Benefit in instalments over 5 years instead of a lump sum.

Loan & Paid-Up

Loan: Available after payment of 2 full years' premiums.

Paid-Up Policy: If less than 2 years' premiums paid, policy lapses. If >2 years paid, it continues with reduced benefits.
