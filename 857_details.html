Jeevan Akshay-VII

Plan No. 857

Immediate Pension, Lifelong Security.

LIC’s Jeevan Akshay VII is an immediate annuity plan that can be purchased by paying a lump sum as a Single Premium. Pension starts immediately, providing financial independence for life.

Premium ModeSingle Premium (Lump Sum)
Annuity IntervalsMonthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Yearly
Min Entry Age25 Years (Completed)
Max Entry Age100 Yrs (Option F) / 85 Yrs (Others)
Min Purchase Price₹1 Lakh (Offline) / ₹1.5 Lakh (Online)
Max Purchase PriceNo Upper Limit

Available Annuity Options

  • (i) Annuity for life.
  • (ii) Annuity guaranteed for 5, 10, 15 or 20 years and for life thereafter.
  • (iii) Annuity for life with return of purchase price on death.
  • (iv) Annuity for life increasing at a simple rate of 3% p.a.
  • (v) Annuity for life with provision for 50% of the annuity to the spouse on death of the annuitant.
  • (vi) Annuity for life with provision for 100% of the annuity to the spouse on death of the annuitant.
  • (vii) Annuity for life with 100% annuity to spouse and return of purchase price on death of last survivor.

Death Benefit Structure

Option (i), (iv): Payment of annuity ceases immediately.

Option (ii): If death occurs during guarantee period, annuity continues to nominee till end of period; ceases if death occurs after.

Option (iii): Annuity ceases and Full Purchase Price is returned to the nominee.

Option (v) & (vi): Annuity ceases, then 50% or 100% (as per option) is paid to the surviving spouse for life.

Option (vii): 100% annuity to surviving spouse for life; Purchase Price returned to nominee after spouse's death.
