Plan No. 774

Amritbaal

LIC’s Amritbaal is a Non-Linked, Non-Participating, Individual, Savings, Life Insurance Plan. The plan is specially designed to build an adequate corpus to meet the higher education and other needs of your child.

Key Specifications

Entry Age

Min: 0 years (30 days)
Max: 13 years (Last Birthday)

Policy Term

Limited: 10 to 25 Years
Single: 5 to 25 Years

Maturity Age

Min: 18 years
Max: 25 years

Premium Payment

Single Premium or
Limited: 5, 6, or 7 Years

Sum Assured

Min: ₹2,00,00,000
Max: No Limit (Based on Income)

Core Benefits

Survival / Maturity Benefit

On survival till the date of maturity, the policyholder receives a guaranteed lump sum, which consists of:

  • Basic Sum Assured
  • Accrued Guaranteed Additions

This maturity amount is paid in one lump sum to meet the child’s professional studies or long-term goals.

Death Benefit

Before Commencement of Risk:

Total premiums paid (excluding taxes, extra premium, and rider premium) shall be payable.

After Commencement of Risk:

Sum of Sum Assured on Death (based on the option chosen at the time of purchase).

Note: Benefit shall not be less than 105% of the total premiums paid as on date of death.

Surrender Value

Limited Premium:

The policy can be surrendered at any time provided premiums have been paid for full two years.

Single Premium:

The policy can be surrendered at any time during the policy term.

Loan Facility

Limited Premium:

Available after the payment of premiums for at least two full years.

Single Premium:

Available after three months from the date of policy issuance.

Tax Benefits

Income Tax Rebate: Premiums paid under this plan are eligible for TAX rebate under Section 80C.

Tax-Free Maturity: Maturity amounts under this plan are free from tax under Section 10(10D) as per prevailing laws.
